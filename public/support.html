Getting started

  1. In the Ring app, open Ring Appstore, find Visitor Recognizer, review the permissions and choose the cameras to share.
  2. Ring opens our sign-in page. Choose Create account (this is a new account, separate from your Ring account) or sign in if you already have one.
  3. You'll see “Account linked successfully” and your cameras. Select Go to dashboard.
  4. Press your doorbell: the arrival appears under Arrivals within about a minute.

Facial recognition (optional)

  1. On the dashboard, under Facial recognition, read the explanation, tick the box and select Turn on facial recognition.
  2. Under Enroll a person, enter their name, add a clear photo showing only their face (or use Enroll this person on an arrival), choose how they agreed, confirm, and select Enroll.
  3. Their next arrival shows their name. Everyone else shows as Unknown.
  4. To remove someone, select Delete next to their name. To stop entirely, select Turn off and delete all face templates.

Common questions

No arrivals are showing

Ring only sends motion inside your motion zones, and Smart Alerts can filter motion types. Open the Ring app, go to your device settings, and check Motion Settings and Motion Zones. Make sure your Ring Protection plan is active and the camera is online.

“Encrypted video” or “No snapshot”

If Ring video encryption is on for a camera, apps can't read its snapshots, so the arrival is listed without a picture. “No snapshot” means Ring had no image for that moment.

Recognition isn't offered for my camera

Facial recognition isn't available for cameras located in Illinois, Texas or Washington because of state biometric laws. Arrivals still work.

A name was wrong

Recognition can make mistakes. Re-enroll the person with a brighter, front-facing photo, or delete the enrollment.

Change cameras or disconnect

Open the Ring app → Ring Appstore → My Apps → Visitor Recognizer to change cameras or remove the app.

Connect a different Ring account

Each Visitor Recognizer account connects to one Ring account. To switch, open your dashboard, go to Ring connection, select Stop using this Ring account here, then connect the other Ring account from the Ring app. Or use a separate Visitor Recognizer account for it.

Forgot password

Email us from your account address and we'll reset it.

Delete my data

Dashboard → Privacy and your data → Delete all my data, or Delete my account.
