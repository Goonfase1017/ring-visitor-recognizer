The service

Visitor Recognizer is a hosted web app that works with Ring cameras through the Ring Appstore. It shows an arrival log with snapshots from the cameras you share, and, if you turn it on, names people you have enrolled. It is an independent app, not made or endorsed by Ring. You need a Ring account, a Ring doorbell or camera, and a Ring Protection plan. You must be 18 or older.

Your responsibilities

  • Only connect cameras you own or are authorized to manage, and follow laws on recording and notice where you live.
  • Only enroll a person after they have agreed to it. Delete their enrollment if they ask.
  • Do not use Visitor Recognizer to stalk, harass, track or monitor anyone, to identify strangers, to build watchlists, or for any unlawful purpose. We may suspend accounts that do.
  • Keep your password private.

Recognition limits

Recognition can be wrong because of lighting, angle, image quality, ageing or look-alikes. Results are informational, not identity verification. Do not rely on Visitor Recognizer as a security, alarm, emergency or child-safety system, or to make decisions about people.

The service is provided “as is”. Events, snapshots and recognition may be delayed or unavailable, for example when Ring has no snapshot, a camera is offline, or a camera uses Ring video encryption.
