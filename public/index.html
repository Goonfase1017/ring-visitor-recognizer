Know who came to your door

Visitor Recognizer keeps a simple arrival log for your Ring doorbell and cameras: every doorbell press and motion event, with a snapshot. If you choose, it can also name the people you enroll yourself, such as family and regular visitors.

A hosted web app. Nothing to install. Compatible with Ring cameras and doorbells.

Create account Sign in

Arrival log

Each doorbell press or motion event on the cameras you share appears with a snapshot, the camera name and the time. Works without facial recognition.

Recognition only if you turn it on

Facial recognition is off by default. You turn it on with a separate, explicit consent and enroll each person yourself. Anyone you haven't enrolled is shown as “Unknown”, and nothing is kept about them.

You stay in control

Delete an enrolled person, any arrival, all your data or your whole account at any time, with confirmation. Arrivals and snapshots delete themselves after 30 days.

Private by design

Processing happens on the Visitor Recognizer server, hosted on Render in the United States. Everything is encrypted at rest. Your data is never sold, shared with other customers, or used for ads.

How it works

  1. In the Ring app, open Ring Appstore, find Visitor Recognizer and choose the cameras to share.
  2. Ring sends you here: create a Visitor Recognizer account (separate from your Ring account) or sign in.
  3. Your arrivals start appearing on your dashboard. Optionally turn on facial recognition and enroll the people you want named.

Requires a Ring account with at least one Ring doorbell or camera and a Ring Protection plan (needed for snapshots). Cameras with Ring video encryption turned on can't share snapshots with apps.
