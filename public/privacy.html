Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026. Visitor Recognizer is an independent app by Aoi Corp. It works with Ring cameras through the Ring Appstore but is not made or endorsed by Ring.

Where your data is processed and stored

All face detection, face-template (embedding) generation, and matching run server-side on the app's cloud service, hosted on Render (render.com) in the United States. Processing does not run on your phone or camera, and no other third-party face-recognition service is used. The app uses the open MobileFaceNet model, run by the app's own server.

The following is stored in the app service's data directory on Render: visitor names, registration photos, face templates (embeddings), consent records, arrival events with snapshots, and an access audit log. Everything except the audit log is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written to disk, using a key held only in the service's environment configuration. Temporary image copies used while processing are deleted right after a template is made.

Biometric data

A face template is created only for a person the account owner registers, and only after the owner confirms that person has consented. Unregistered visitors seen by the camera are compared against registered templates; no template is kept for them. Their snapshot is kept as an event for up to 30 days unless deleted sooner. Biometric data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is never used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Consent

Before a visitor can be registered, the owner must tick a consent statement confirming the person agreed, and choose how consent was given (verbal, written, or "this is me"). The app records the time, method, and exact statement with the visitor record. Registration is refused without it.

Opting out and deletion

A registered visitor can ask the owner to opt them out at any time. Opt-out immediately deletes their face template and photo, and they are no longer matched. The owner can also delete a visitor, any event, or all data at once, and can export all stored data. Deletions remove the encrypted files from the service right away.

Retention

Arrival events and snapshots are deleted automatically 30 days after capture by an hourly retention job. Visitor records are kept until the owner deletes them or the visitor opts out. Audit-log entries are kept for 12 months. Unlinking the Ring account or emailing us lets us delete the account's data.

Ring account access

The app uses Ring authorization to read camera and doorbell events and snapshots for the owner's devices only. It does not access Ring video for any other purpose, and no person reviews your video.

Notifications

If the owner turns on alerts, notification text (for example "Visitor has arrived") is sent through Amazon SNS. No photos or face templates are included in alerts.

Security

Personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS). All data APIs require the owner's access token, and every access is written to an audit log. See our Security page for vulnerability management and incident response.
