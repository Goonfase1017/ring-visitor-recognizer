Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026

Data stored by the app

Ring Visitor Recognizer stores visitor names, uploaded photos, generated face embeddings, arrival event metadata, and Ring camera snapshots locally in the application's data directory. It does not sell or upload this data to a central service operated by the app.

Ring account access

The app connects to your Ring account using credentials that you provide, including a Ring refresh token. Those credentials and access tokens are used to retrieve camera information and snapshots. Keep your refresh token private and do not share it.

Optional AWS notifications

If configured by the owner, the app may send alert data to AWS SNS, including notification text and an optional SMS recipient number. AWS processes those alerts under the AWS account configured by the app owner.

Deletion and retention

Use the dashboard to delete individual known visitors and unrecognized event records. Deleting a known visitor removes its local name, photo, and embedding. Deleting an unrecognized event removes its event record and saved snapshot where available.

Security

Run the app only on systems and networks you trust. Protect the local data directory, environment variables, refresh token, AWS credentials, and dashboard access. Face recognition is an identification aid and should not be used as a sole security decision mechanism.

Contact

Use the app's Support page for assistance.
