Visitor RecognizerWorks with Ring cameras

Ring connection

Motion events arrive only for motion inside your camera's motion zones, and Smart Alerts can filter which motion is sent. If arrivals are missing, open the Ring app, go to your device settings, and check Motion Settings and Motion Zones. To change which cameras are shared or to remove this app, open the Ring app and go to Ring Appstore → My Apps → Visitor Recognizer.

Arrivals

Each doorbell press or motion event, with a snapshot. Deleted automatically after 30 days.

Facial recognition

Optional. When it's on, arrivals show the names of people you enroll yourself. Everyone else is shown as “Unknown”, and nothing is kept about them. The arrival log works without it.

What turning it on means

  • What's collected: a face template (numbers that describe facial geometry) for each person you enroll. The photo you use is not kept.
  • Why: only to show you who arrived at your door.
  • Where: processed on the Visitor Recognizer server (Render, United States) and stored encrypted in the Neon database in the US.
  • Which cameras: only the cameras you share from your one connected Ring account, including cameras at other addresses in that same Ring account. Each Visitor Recognizer account connects to one Ring account, and your enrollments are never used with any other Ring account.
  • How long: until you delete the person or turn recognition off (or one year after your last sign-in). Turning it off permanently deletes every template.
  • Never: sold, shared, used for ads, or compared with anyone else's enrollments.

Privacy and your data

We keep: your account email, a link to your Ring account (encrypted), your camera names, arrivals with snapshots (30 days), face templates of people you enroll (only if recognition is on), and this activity log. Read the Privacy Policy.
